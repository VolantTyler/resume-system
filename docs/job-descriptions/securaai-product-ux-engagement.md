Source: https://securaai.com/

## Mission

SecuraAI is an AI security and governance platform: discover every AI and agent across the enterprise (including shadow AI), test it against real-world attacks, and prove it is safe with audit-ready evidence.

Mission (company): empower organizations to deploy and adopt AI with the highest standards of safety, security, privacy, and ethical integrity.

Platform themes: agentic AI security, human approval / step-up controls, policy and guardrails, risk scoring, monitoring and evidence, conversational AI safety, red teaming, model risk scanning, continuous discovery and governance.

Framework alignment language used by the company: OWASP LLM Top 10, NIST AI RMF, MITRE ATLAS, EU AI Act, ISO 42001.

## Engagement brief (what this résumé should signal)

Primary (product & UX): someone who can make AI security products usable and trustworthy for operators, security teams, and business stakeholders—clear workflows for discovery, risk, human approval, and proof; accessible interfaces; design systems; and human-centered controls when model confidence is low.

Secondary (GTM / customer-facing): comfort translating ambiguous customer needs into working software, partnering with marketing/product leadership, and supporting demos, discovery conversations, and design-partner style delivery for a startup hungry for new customers.
