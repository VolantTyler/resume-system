# Front-End Engineer, QA-Minded — Example Nonprofit Org

We're a mission-driven nonprofit technology team looking for a front-end engineer
who cares about release reliability as much as UI implementation.

## What you'll do

- Build and maintain user-facing features in our Laravel/Livewire donor and event
  platforms.
- Own Cypress regression testing for critical user workflows and document test
  coverage for the team.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to ship reliable releases, including staging
  validation and release support.
- Improve accessibility and UI quality across our nonprofit-facing products.

## What we're looking for

- Experience with Laravel, Livewire, and Alpine.js in production applications.
- Hands-on Cypress or similar regression testing experience.
- A track record of platform maintenance and practical engineering in
  mission-driven or nonprofit settings.
- Comfort with JavaScript, HTML/CSS, and release documentation.

Bonus: experience with design systems or accessibility (WCAG) work.
